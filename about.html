04 / about us

A story written
in the little things.

It all started with something so simple — just a little “Hi.” At the time, neither of us could have imagined that one small message would slowly become such a beautiful part of our lives.

One conversation led to another, and before we knew it, the random messages turned into long conversations, shared laughter, little updates about our days, and countless moments that somehow started to mean more than we expected.

Somewhere along the way, you stopped being just someone I met through a message and became someone I looked forward to talking to, someone I could share my little thoughts with, and someone who made ordinary days feel a little more special. What began as a simple hello slowly became comfort, trust, memories, and a bond that feels truly ours.

We didn’t know the story we were beginning that day; we were simply living it, one conversation and one memory at a time. And now, every little moment feels like another page in a story I’m grateful we get to write together.

Maybe the most beautiful stories don’t begin with anything extraordinary. Sometimes, they begin with just a “Hi.”

And ours is still being written, one little frame, one little memory, and one beautiful moment at a time.

From a simple “Hi” to something we call ours.
And this is only the beginning.

Looking back, it’s funny to think that something as small as a “Hi” could lead us to all these beautiful moments.

♡

There is no perfect version of us. There is only this beautiful, ordinary, growing thing we keep choosing every day.
